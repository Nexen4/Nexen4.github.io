Privacy policy

FPV Drone Frontlines · Neoth4 (Nemanja Milosavljevic)

Updated 2026-11-04

Provider privacy information: Google · Epic Games · GitHub.

Who operates the game

FPV Drone Frontlines is operated by Neoth4 (Nemanja Milosavljevic), Serbia. For privacy questions, support, moderation appeals or requests concerning your data, contact nexen44@gmail.com.

Local game data

Career progress, earned items, control profiles, preferences, maps and saved replays are stored on your device. The game does not operate a separate cloud-save service for your career. You can remove local files through your device's app storage controls or by deleting the files you exported. Uninstalling may not remove files you separately exported or copies retained by your device backup service.

Internet multiplayer

Epic Online Services provides online identity, room discovery and peer connections. A device-based product user identifier is used without requiring you to sign in to an Epic account. The service processes network and device information needed to establish and maintain connections. Your chosen pilot name, selected picture and colour, room information, gameplay state and any maps you share are transmitted to participants and service providers as needed for online play. Other players may see or save content you share.

Chat, microphone and reports

Text chat is sent to room participants; recent messages are kept in memory for the session. Voice starts off, requires microphone permission and is sent to the room only when you turn it on. New operation replays record game sounds and music, excluding voice chat. The game does not create separate voice-chat recordings. This does not remove audio from replays saved in older builds. The block list is stored on your device. If you submit a report, the reported player's service identifier, your service identifier, category, optional description and relevant game context are sent through Epic's reporting service for moderation. A selected chat message is included when you explicitly report that message. Support correspondence and moderation reports may be reviewed by the operator.

Advertising

The Android game uses Google AdMob. Depending on your privacy choices and applicable settings, Google's ads SDK processes identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, ad/app interactions and diagnostic information for ads, measurement and fraud prevention. The game does not request GPS location permission. Where required, a consent form is shown before ad requests are permitted. You can revisit available consent choices from Settings > Privacy & Rules. Android advertising-ID controls are available in your device settings.

Purchases

Google Play handles Android purchases and payment details. The game processes purchase records, purchase tokens and entitlement information to verify and restore purchases. We do not receive your full payment-card details. Remove Ads disables automatic ads; optional ads for bonus points remain available. Google may retain transaction records under its own policies and legal obligations.

Purposes and choices

Data is used to run the features you choose, deliver and measure advertising, verify purchases, provide support, investigate abuse and protect the service. Where applicable, processing relies on providing the requested service, your consent, legitimate interests in security and service operation, or legal obligations. Multiplayer, microphone use, shared maps and rewarded ads are optional. Removing ads does not remove processing necessary for multiplayer or purchases.

Recipients and international processing

Relevant recipients include Google for advertising and purchases, Epic Games for online services and reports, other players for room content, and the operator for support and moderation. These services may process data in countries other than your own. Their handling is also explained in Google's and Epic's privacy information linked below. Public support pages are hosted by GitHub, which processes web requests under its own privacy statement. No separate analytics SDK has been added to the game beyond the disclosed services.

Retention and deletion

Local saves remain until you remove them. The game clears its room chat history when you leave the room and keeps transient voice buffers only for playback. Saved maps and replays remain until you delete them. Providers retain service, advertising and purchase records according to their policies and applicable law. Support messages and moderation reports are retained only as needed to handle the request, enforce rules, protect against abuse and meet legal obligations. Data may need to be retained for fraud prevention, disputes or legal compliance; any applicable exception will be explained when handling a deletion request.

Your rights and requests

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your data, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent, or complain to your local data-protection authority. Email nexen44@gmail.com with the subject FPV Drone Frontlines privacy request. You can request deletion through the dedicated data-deletion page, including after uninstalling. We may ask for proportionate information to identify the affected data. Do not send passwords, payment-card numbers or identity documents with an initial request.

Children and changes

The game contains combat and user communication and is not designed for young children. Follow the age rating and audience information displayed for your country on Google Play. If you believe a child has supplied personal information, contact us. We may update this policy when features or providers change; the current version and its date are published here.
