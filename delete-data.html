Request deletion of your online identity and associated data

Email nexen44@gmail.com with the subject FPV Drone Frontlines data deletion. State that you want your online identity and associated personal data deleted. Include your pilot name and, if you still have it, your online support identifier, approximate last-play date or relevant room details. You can make a request after uninstalling and do not need to reinstall the game.

What happens next

We will review your request, use proportionate steps to identify the correct data and arrange deletion of associated data held by the operator and relevant game-service records where applicable. If additional information is needed, we will explain why. We will explain completion and any limited data that must remain for legal obligations, fraud prevention or dispute handling. A deletion request is not merely account suspension.

Local saves and store records

To delete career progress, locally saved maps, replays and settings, use your device's app-storage controls and remove any separately exported files. A support request cannot remotely delete files on your device or copies saved by other players. Google handles store transaction records and refund requests under its own rules. Deleting game data does not automatically refund a purchase.
